# Job Title: Director of Finance and Business Systems

## Position Summary

We are seeking a pro-active, results-oriented, deadline driven professional with broad experience in all aspects of accounting and financial management. The ideal candidate is someone who sees the big picture and plans appropriate strategies to achieve results. You will serve as a financial lead committed to strategic management and quality performance, partnering with organization leaders to effectively align with and support key initiatives. Additionally, this role oversees all aspects of the organization's quality or business improvement efforts, including developing programs and facilitating change throughout the organization.

## Core Responsibilities

**Financial Management & Reporting**
* Maintain overall authority for budget preparation and administration, audit, accounting, monthly closing of books, account reconciliation, and journal entry adjustments.
* Act as the financial and accounting lead for the division responsible for P&L performance, achieving EBTIA and working capital targets.
* Establish and maintain internal controls according to GAAP and SOX for annual audits.
* Prepare monthly, quarterly and annual reports summarizing the business activities.
* Evaluate inventory levels, monitor COGS, and ensure raw material price fluctuations are inline with product pricing.


**Operations & Information Systems**

* Serve as the business process owner for SAP software implementation.
* Administer the installation, configuration and continued maintenance of network equipment including servers, storage devices, routers, hubs, and switches.
* Implement new sales-tracking POS systems to replace manual form writing processes to increase efficiency.
* Manage all aspects of Supply Chain, which includes developing and implementing purchasing, operations, logistics and production systems.
* Develop and maintain interactive sales reporting and analysis systems used by marketing, sales managers and operational departments.



**Team Leadership & Human Resources**

* Build and retain high performance teams by hiring, developing and motivating skilled professionals.
* Perform the full gamut of human resource tasks from hiring to termination, ensuring company compliance with state and federal regulations.
* Supervise administrative functions, inventory, cash flow, merchandising, and operations.
* Introduce monthly team meetings to enhance team environment, review projects and provide training.
* Clearly and effectively communicate direction, commit people to action with timelines, and transform complex information into easily understood formats.



## Qualifications & Technical Skills

* A degree in Accounting, Business Administration, or Management Information Systems.
* Comprehensive proficiency with financial and enterprise software, including SAP, Great Plains, Quickbooks, SQL, Access, and Database management.
* Strong technical background with knowledge of the Software Development Life Cycle, network security, and disaster recovery.
* Proven expertise in organizational re-engineering, product life cycle management, and continuous process improvements.
* Experience negotiating and closing contracts, including vendor agreements and MDA negotiations.
